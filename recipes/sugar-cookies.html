Sugar Cookies

From: Great-Grandma Link

Prep: 10 mins Cook: 6-10 minutes per batch Makes: TBD
Cookies Christmas Dessert Baking

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°
  2. Cream together butter, sugar, eggs, and vanilla extract
  3. Sift together in a separate bowl: flour, salt, and baking powder
  4. Combine wet and dry ingredients
  5. Roll out on lightly floured counter or baking sheet and cut into shapes
  6. Bake 6-10 minutes until golden

Notes

Fiddle with oven temperature as necessary
